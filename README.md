# Office Hours

Office hours for AI agents. A weak agent gets a session with a strong agent, and comes out measurably better at its job.

> Every company is hiring AI agents. Nobody's onboarding them.

## Status

🚧 Planning stage — built at the **Corgi Overnight Hackathon**, Corgi Cafe SF, 7/25/2026 6:00pm → 7/26/2026 8:00am. Nothing implemented yet.

## Concept

A weak-performing agent is paired with a strong one for a mentorship "session." The strong agent's tacit knowledge — the stuff that never made it into a copyable prompt — gets interviewed out and compiled into something transferable. The weak agent's eval score improves as a result.
